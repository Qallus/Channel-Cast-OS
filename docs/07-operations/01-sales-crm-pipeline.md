# Sales CRM Pipeline

## Lead Types

- Advertiser lead
- Business/ad-space owner lead
- Reseller lead
- Partner lead
- Radio station lead
- Voice talent lead
- Installation partner lead
- Enterprise campaign lead

## Pipeline Stages

1. New lead
2. Qualified
3. Demo scheduled
4. Proposal sent
5. Contract/booking pending
6. Payment pending
7. Onboarding
8. Active
9. Renewal/expansion

## Lead Fields

- Company
- Contact
- Email
- Phone
- Type
- Geography
- Budget
- Interest
- Source
- Notes
- Assigned user
- Next follow-up date
- Status
